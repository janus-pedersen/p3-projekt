\subsubsection{Sensio}

Sensio was founded in Norway in 2009 and today serves customers in Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Iceland, England, Germany, and France.
The company primarily works with municipalities and the public sector to enhance safety and security in nursing homes and hospitals. However, their solutions can also be used in private homes for those who want extra security in their everyday lives \cite{noauthor_om_nodate}. 

Among Sensio’s solutions are RoomMate and Safemate, both developed to create greater safety for the elderly while supporting the work of care staff.

RoomMate (as seen in \autoref{fig:sensioRoomMate}) is an advanced sensor placed in a room that detects falls and other potentially dangerous situations. When an incident is detected, an alarm is immediately sent to the care staff, without the resident needing to wear any equipment or call for help themselves. The solution allows staff to respond quickly without unnecessarily disturbing the resident. In addition to falls, the sensor also detects loud sounds, for example if a resident calls for help \cite{noauthor_roommate_nodate}.

Safemate (as seen in \autoref{fig:sensioSafemate}) is a safety alarm with a simple button that the user can activate in an emergency. It is equipped with \gls{gps}, allowing care staff or relatives to see the user’s location via an app. When the alarm is triggered, a notification is immediately sent to the relevant people, who can then provide the necessary assistance. Safemate is aimed at seniors who want to stay longer in their own homes with an added sense of security. However, the product is not available for private purchase but is provided through the municipality \cite{noauthor_safemate_nodate}.

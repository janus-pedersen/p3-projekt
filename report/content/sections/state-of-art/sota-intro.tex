The purpose of this section is to map the existing technological developments within safety alarms and fall detection systems, with the aim of identifying relevant trends, solutions, and challenges in the current market. An analysis of the existing “state of the art” forms the basis for the project’s further problem understanding and design decisions, as it provides insight into the technologies, functionalities, and areas of application that characterize the field today.

The section focuses on a selection of products and systems that represent different approaches to handling fall incidents and care technology. Particular emphasis is placed on parameters such as functionality, target demographic, technological infrastructure, user-friendliness, and the degree of integration with existing care and safety systems.

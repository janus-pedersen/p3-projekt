\subsection{State of art}

The following section describes the methods used in the state of art section, sorted into what parts of the state of art that the methods were used. 

\subsubsection{Existing solutions}
For the existing solutions part of the state of art, a verbal brainstorm was conducted based on any existing and known solutions for fall detection of elderly people. From this a few products were found, which was searched for on the internet, so that the name of the products and companies were found. 

\subsubsection{Existing algorithms}
In finding other existing algorithms, the methodology was using Google Scholar with the search 'Fall detection'. Then the search result were sorted by what technology was used for the fall detection. For the project, the focus is fall detection using an accelerometer and gyroscope, thus other existing solutions with said technology were chosen in favour of other solutions. Futhermore litterature review articles was used to find the newest articles published regarding the field along with finding more articles than first found with the previously mentioned keywords. The litterature reviews also helped give an overview of what had happened in the field the past years.

\subsection{State of art}

The following section describes the methods used in the state of art section, sorted into what parts of the state of art that the methods were used. 

\subsubsection{Existing solutions}
For the existing solutions section of the state of art analysis, a verbal brainstorming process was conducted based on known and commercially available fall detection solutions for elderly individuals. The methodology involved conducting Google searches using the keywords \textit{"fall detection alarm"}, \textit{"fall detection for elderly"}, \textit{"Smartwatch fall detection"} and \textit{"Emergency buttons health care"}. Based on these searches, several relevant products were identified, after which further online research was carried out to determine the names of the products and the companies behind them.

Based on the various search results, a number of companies were selected that work with fall detection in different ways and whose solutions vary widely in both functionality and target groups. The selection aimed to provide an overview of existing approaches and to serve as an inspirational foundation for the design of the solution presented in this report, including clarification of whether the primary target group should be the municipality or the individual citizen.

\subsubsection{Existing algorithms}
In finding other existing algorithms, the methodology was using Google Scholar with the search 'Fall detection'. Then the search result were sorted by what technology was used for the fall detection. For the project, the focus is fall detection using an accelerometer and gyroscope, thus other existing solutions with said technology were chosen in favor of other solutions. Furthermore this literature review article, \textit{The Methods of Fall Detection: A Literature Review} \cite{newaz_methods_2023}, was used to find articles published regarding the field along with finding more articles than first found with the previously mentioned keywords. The literature reviews also helped give an overview of what had happened in the field the past years.

An attempt was also made to establish contact with Sensorem in order to conduct an interview regarding their design choices and the algorithms used for fall detection. However, this was not possible, and the analysis of algorithms has therefore been based exclusively on scientific literature within the field.

\subsection{Introduction}

The  methodology for the introduction began with the search 'ældrebyrden i Danmark' (the burden of the elderly in Denmark). After that, Ældresagens databases 'Analyser og undersøgelser om ældre' () and 'Ældre i tal' (elderly people in numbers) was found. Where the following keywords were used to search for further articles and statistics: 'demografi', 'hjemmehjælp og rehabilitering', 'dagligdag', 'pårørende', 'sundhedsvæsen og velfærdsteknologi' ('demography', 'domiciliary care and rehabilitation','everyday life', 'relatives', healthcare and wefare technology'). Furthermore the group was sent some articles from Ældresagen in a mail regarding a possible interview; these were picked by a consultant at Ældresagen.

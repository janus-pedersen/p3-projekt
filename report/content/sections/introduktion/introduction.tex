Statistics Denmark (Danmarks Statestik) made a population projection in 2018, which showed that the growth of the population in Denmark primarily have to do with the increase of elderly people. The demographic group of 65-79-year-olds is expected to be 7\% larger than today for next ten years, while the group of people over 80 is expected to be 58\% larger than today. According to the population projection, the amount of elderly people over 80 will grow to 2.5 times larger than today, compared by 2057. This means that it is expected that every tenth citizen after 2053 is older than 80\cite{noauthor_nyt_nodate}. The interesseorganisation Ældre sagen, wrote an article in 2023, in which they state that the demographic group of 65+ year-olds in Denmark take up a fifth of the population\cite{noauthor_aeldre_nodate}.

\subsection{Accuracy, Sensitivity, and Specificity}

In order to test the algoritm's accuracy, i.e. its sensitivity and specificity, a series of simulations were made. 

Initially the test of the algorithm was tested with an impact value of 4g. As seen in table ? a total of 15 simulations were tested. Of said simulations only two out of ten falls were detected as positive, the eight rest were not detected at all. These tests were done landing on hard ground, a softer ground, while landing on multiple sides. In terms of testing for false positives four out of five simulations tested negative while also predicting so. These tests were made to simulate respectively roughly sitting down in a chair, practicing yoga, doing boxing and picking vegetables. The simulation, which was a false positive was done while simulating going on a run. 

After the previous tests, the impact value was changed to a value of 3g, in which five fall simulations were made on soft ground. Out of the five falls, none of them tested positive by the algorithm, therefore testes for false positives was not conducted.

\subsection{Wristband design}
The wristband is designed as the primary sensing unit of the system and therefore plays a critical role in both functionality and user acceptance. As the device is intended to be worn continuously, comfort, size, and simplicity were key design considerations.

The wrist-worn form factor was chosen based on findings from the State of Art analysis, where wrist-based solutions were shown to be widely accepted and familiar to users. A wristband resembles a conventional watch, which reduces stigmatization and increases the likelihood of consistent use.

The wristband is designed to operate independently of user interaction during normal operation. The user is not required to initiate measurements or confirm system status, except in cases where the emergency button is intentionally pressed or a false alarm must be cancelled through the mobile application.

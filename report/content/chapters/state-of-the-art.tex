\subsection{Existing Solutions}

For the existing solutions part of the state of art, a verbal brainstorm was conducted based on any existing and known solutions for fall detection of elderly people. From this a few products were found, we then searched on google, google scholar, IEEE, as well as AUB.

The described solutions (Smart watches, Cekura, Intelligent Care, Sensorem, and Sensio) together illustrate a broad range of both commercial and institutional products. The review aims to uncover both the strengths and limitations of current solutions, thereby identifying potential areas for development that can form the basis for the project’s own technological and conceptual innovation.

\subsection{Existing Algorithms}
In finding other existing algorithms, the methodology was using Google Scholar with the search 'Fall detection'. Then the search result were sorted by what technology was used for the fall detection. For the project, the focus is fall detection using an accelerometer, thus other existing solutions with said technology were chosen in favor of other solutions.
